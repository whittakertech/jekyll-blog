This page describes what happens to your data when you visit whittakertech.com. It covers the site
only. WhittakerTech's gems and documentation sites are separate.

## The short version

- The site works without any analytics. Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity load **only if you accept** them in the banner.
- I use the data to see which pages and posts are useful, and to fix broken pages. I don't sell it.
- You can change your mind at any time with **Cookie settings** in the footer.

## What is collected, and by whom

### Analytics you can accept or decline

- **Google Analytics 4 (Google).** Page views, approximate location, device and browser details, and how you arrived. It sets cookies in your browser.
- **Microsoft Clarity (Microsoft).** Anonymised session recordings and heatmaps that show how pages are used: clicks, scrolling, and movement. Clarity masks sensitive input fields by default. It sets cookies in your browser.

If you decline, or your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, neither loads.

### Always on

- **Cloudflare.** The site is served through Cloudflare, which processes requests (including your IP address) to deliver the pages and protect against abuse. I also use **Cloudflare Web Analytics**, which measures page views without cookies or tracking you across sites.
- **GitHub Pages.** The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs.
- **Security reports.** The site tells your browser which sources it may load content from (a content security policy). If your browser sees something that breaks that policy, it may send a short technical report to an error-tracking service (GlitchTip) that I run on my own server. The report contains the page address, the address of the blocked item and your browser's user agent, and your IP address may be recorded with it. I use these reports only to find and fix problems with the site, and I don't share them. They are sent whether or not you accepted analytics, because they are a security measure, not tracking.

### Forms

- **Email sign-up.** If you enter your email address in the newsletter form, it is sent to **Formspree**, a form-handling service, and delivered to me. I use it only to tell you about new writing. I keep the list by hand, so to be removed, email me and I'll delete your address.

## Cookies and local storage

- `wt-consent` (local storage on this site): remembers your analytics choice.
- Google Analytics and Clarity cookies: only after you accept.

## Retention

Analytics data is kept according to each provider's retention settings. Security reports are kept only as long as needed to fix the problem they point to. Form submissions are kept until you ask me to delete them.

## Your choices

- Use **Cookie settings** in the footer to accept or decline analytics again.
- Browser settings and Global Privacy Control are respected.
- To ask what I hold about you, or to have it deleted, email [{{ site.author.email }}](mailto:{{ site.author.email }}).

I'll update this page when the site's tracking changes. Last updated: 1 October 2026.
