The founder

Lee Whittaker Founder & engineer

Lee has spent more than ten years building production Rails systems: rescuing legacy apps, chasing data-integrity bugs that passing tests didn't catch, and making pipelines faster without making them bigger. The engines are the opinions that work left behind.

Before software, Lee worked in visual effects and video compositing. That's why WhittakerTech engines arrive with brand systems, icons, and documentation sites instead of a README and a shrug. He also runs the studio's infrastructure himself, from CI to documentation builds to error tracking, on hardware he maintains.

His consulting work, client case studies, and career history live on his personal site.
