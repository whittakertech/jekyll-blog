The gap we work in

Great tools stay unopinionated. Someone has to have the opinions.

Good libraries stay neutral so they can serve everyone. That's the right call for them, and it leaves every team to reinvent the same integration decisions, usually under deadline and usually slightly wrong.

Each WhittakerTech engine picks one of those domains and ships the missing opinions as a gem you can install.
